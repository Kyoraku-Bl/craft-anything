What if you changed your target?

Replays your closed trades against the loaded bars with a different target R, keeping the original stop and size. Stop is always checked before target in the same candle.

Local economic calendar

Add your own historical events (from your broker, a CSV or a licensed calendar). They stay in this browser and appear as markers on the chart. No news feed is bundled.
